About People & Culture

Connecting professionals and transforming workplace communities

P&C is a zero-trust, cloud-native workplace social networking platform engineered to foster authentic connections, vibrant organizational cultures, and transparent communication across distributed teams and organizations.

Our Mission

Empower professionals to discover insights, exchange workplace knowledge, recognize team achievements, and drive meaningful growth in their organizations.

Core Capabilities

  • Interactive Feeds & Facts: Share professional updates, project milestones, verified facts, and multimedia stories.
  • Direct Cloudflare R2 Uploads: High-performance media delivery without server bandwidth bottlenecks.
  • Enterprise Security: Complete backend data validation, zero-trust REST APIs, and modern session safeguards.
  • Real-time Co-worker Connections: Discover and collaborate with peers and team members globally.
