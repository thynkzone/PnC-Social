About People & Culture (P&C)

P&C is a workplace social networking platform dedicated to fostering meaningful connections, vibrant organizational cultures, and transparent communication among teams and professionals.

Our Mission

Empower employees and workplace communities to share insights, celebrate accomplishments, collaborate on ideas, and inspire positive changes within their organizations.

Core Features

From company updates and memes to professional networking and peer recognition, P&C brings the fun and connection of modern social tools directly to work life.
